OPTIONS SCAN · option-v1
Runs every 5 minutes from 09:35 ET to the close on trading days, on the underlyings in your scan universe. Each underlying is judged on its at-the-money call and put at the first expiry far enough out (set by the options sweep). Strategies are fixed: buy calls, sell cash-secured puts, sell covered calls. Never buy puts, spreads or naked calls.

LISTING
- List a setup when it scores 70 or more.
- While a name has under 20 days of IV history, list it from 60.
- Show at most 20 ideas, one per symbol and strategy.
- Drop an idea after it misses 2 scans in a row.
- Near misses: not shown.

BUY A CALL
- Contract: the at-the-money call (set by the options sweep).
- Gate · Momentum average: 15-minute and hourly momentum average 40 or more.
- Gate · Both timeframes up: both 15-minute and hourly momentum are above 0.
- Gate · Daily uptrend: daily trend is 25 or more.
- Score: starts at 40.
  - Up to +25 for momentum: the average of 15-minute and hourly momentum from 40 (none) to 100 (full).
  - Up to +10 for daily trend: daily trend from 0 (none) to 50 (full).
  - Up to +15 when options are cheap: IV rank from 60 (none) to 30 (full). With no IV rank yet: full when 30-day IV is at most 1.2x 20-day realised volatility, else none.
  - Up to +5 for volume: relative volume from 0 (none) to 1.5 (full).
  - +5 when relative volume is 1.5x or more (high volume).
- Penalties:
  - -25 when earnings fall before expiry.
  - -10 for each of these signs (stretched), at most -30: price more than 2 fifteen-minute ATRs above VWAP; 15-minute RSI is above 75; today's move is more than 1.5x its usual size.

SELL A CASH-SECURED PUT
- Contract: the at-the-money put (set by the options sweep).
- Gate · Stock worth owning: stock score is 60 or more.
- Gate · IV is rich: IV rank is 60 or more (no IV rank yet: 30-day IV is above 20-day realised volatility).
- Gate · Not falling hard: daily trend is above -50.
- Score: starts at 40.
  - Up to +25 for rich IV: IV rank from 30 (none) to 100 (full). With no IV rank yet: 30-day IV from 0.8x (none) to 1.6x (full) of 20-day realised volatility.
  - Up to +20 for stock score: stock score from 60 (none) to 100 (full).
  - Up to +10 for hourly momentum: hourly momentum (0 when unknown) from -50 (none) to 50 (full).
- Penalties:
  - -15 when earnings fall before expiry.
  - -3 when an ex-dividend date falls before expiry.
- Note, no points: cash needed, when cash to secure a put is above $0.

SELL A COVERED CALL
- Contract: the at-the-money call (set by the options sweep).
- Only against shares you hold, 100 per contract.
- Gate · Shares held: shares held is 100 or more.
- Gate · IV is rich: IV rank is 60 or more (no IV rank yet: 30-day IV is above 20-day realised volatility).
- Gate · 15-minute momentum flat: 15-minute momentum is 20 or less.
- Gate · Hourly momentum flat: hourly momentum is 20 or less.
- Score: starts at 40.
  - Up to +30 for rich IV: IV rank from 30 (none) to 100 (full). With no IV rank yet: 30-day IV from 0.8x (none) to 1.6x (full) of 20-day realised volatility.
  - Up to +20 for flat or falling momentum: the average of 15-minute and hourly momentum from 20 (none) to -60 (full).
  - +10 when daily trend is 20 or less (daily trend flat).
- Penalties:
  - -10 when earnings fall before expiry.
  - -30 when an ex-dividend date falls before expiry.
